Low battery

Vacuum or blow the outside vents first.

Sealed battery

Turn it off. On a wireless alarm, off means off the mount. On a sealed wired alarm, this page does not add another way to turn it off. Hold the test button to drain any power left in it. Turn it back on. Wait 2 to 3 minutes. Battery that comes out

The power is on. Take the alarm off its base. Test the battery with a battery tester. Press the test button while the battery is out, to drain any power left in it. If the tester says the battery is flat, put a new battery in. If it is not flat, put the same battery back. Put the alarm back on its base. Wait 2 to 3 minutes.
