# Install research

Source notes for the technician. Mounting, activation, and opening. Sources are manuals and official pages opened during this pass. This file is not a page on the phone. None of these jobs are in the app.

Mounting is how a wireless alarm goes on its plate. Activation is how a new wireless alarm or a new remote turns on. Opening is how a wired alarm comes off its base and goes back on. Mains wiring stays out. That is electrician work.

## Brooks, wireless alarm, mounting

**Result.** Found in a manual.

**Model.** EiB650iRF. The EiB600 series instruction manual lists EiB650iRF on the model chart. The steps below are the series mounting steps in that manual.

**Source.** https://www.brooks.com.au/index.cfm/_api/render/file/?method=inline&fileID=FA22A3D1-EEB5-4D63-86E266BCE0943C22

**Sheet line.** "Carefully line up the Alarm on the mounting plate, press down gently and twist on."

**Steps.** Twist the mounting plate off the alarm anti-clockwise. Place the plate on the ceiling where the alarm will go, and mark the two screw holes. Drill the marks with a 5.0mm bit, and keep clear of wiring in the ceiling. Push the supplied plastic screw anchors into the holes, and screw the plate to the ceiling. If the alarms use a radio link, pick one side of the building, such as the front wall, and fit every plate in that same orientation. The figure says the large hole goes nearest the front wall. Line the alarm up on the plate, press down gently, and twist it on. The alarm can be made hard to remove by breaking off the small pillar on the back. After that, push the catch towards the ceiling with a screwdriver, then twist the alarm off. A No. 2 or No. 4 self tapping screw, 2 to 3 mm in diameter and 6 to 8 mm long, can lock the alarm to the plate. That screw is not supplied. Fit the alarm to the plate before that screw.

**Manual is silent on.** The sheet says to twist the plate off anti-clockwise, and to twist the alarm on. It does not call the on direction clockwise.
