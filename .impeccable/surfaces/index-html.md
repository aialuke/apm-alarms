# APM Alarms phone screens

Mode: Operate. Scope: every current screen — brands, unit, setup or troubleshooting, topics, and one instruction page. A Remote opens one page of step cards and an RF module opens one page that is only a diagram.

Audience: a smoke alarm technician on a job, one hand, iPhone. Task: pick the brand in hand, then the unit, then the list, then read one page. Wireless pairing is written for ten brands. Wired pairing is written for Cavius, Clipsal, Emerald, Legrand, Matelec, and Siterwell. Testing, lights and sounds, mounting, activation, opening, remote pairing, remote use, and RF fitting are written where a manual gave the steps. A page with no approved words still says they are not written yet. Constraints: dark only, no theme switch, no logos, brand buttons stay three across, Back and Home stay after Brands.

Memorable moment: a brand button answers a press by filling solid blue, and its blue edge stays tight against the shape.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark iPhone cheat sheet whose first screen is a field of large rounded brand buttons, three across. It refuses a text list, a tabbed shell, and a blue box around the later rows.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground. Brand buttons, step cards, notes, and the Back / Home bar have a dark face and a tight blue edge. Unit, Setup or Troubleshooting, and Topics are rows on the black page with only a grey line between them. A blue icon marks a unit, Setup, Troubleshooting, and a shortcut. Topic rows have no icon. The iPhone system font. Steps in cards, with a Tip as a tinted row inside. A Tip is a rounded inset note with the same tight blue edge. A clearance, a wait, a press count, or a confirmed flash interval is Figure Gold (`#f2c14d`). The words around that number stay white. 300 mm and 400 mm use the same gold, in the list and on the ceiling sketch.

STORY: The technician sees the brand, taps it, then walks Unit, Setup or Troubleshooting, Topic, and one scrolling page. A Remote or an RF module is one page straight from the unit. After Brands, the route's current step is the page name, written once and large. The earlier steps stay small. Back and Home stay in the same bottom places.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brands. A large title sits at the top inside the safe area. Below it, a three-column grid of large rounded buttons, the brand name centered in white on a dark face with a tight blue edge. No logos, no bottom bar, no route line. The brand button is the primary action.

FORM: Owner-pinned modern iPhone interface. The tight blue edge is the material of the brand buttons, the note, and the bottom bar. Lists are not boxed. Position: pinned, tournament waived. Seed key: none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
