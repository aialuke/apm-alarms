Placement

Where practical, a smoke alarm goes on the ceiling. Do not place one:

  • within 300 mm of a corner where the ceiling meets a wall
  • within 300 mm of a light fitting
  • within 400 mm of an air-conditioning vent
  • within 400 mm of the blades of a ceiling fan
