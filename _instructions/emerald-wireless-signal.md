<ul class="signals">
<li>Red light once every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, no sound. Standby. The alarm is working.</li>
<li>Flashing red during a false alarm, or just after it. This alarm set the others off.</li>
<li>Red light with the pulse alarm sound every <span class="figure">1</span> second, until you let go. Test.</li>
<li>Yellow light once every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, and <span class="figure">1</span> chirp. Low battery. That warning lasts at least <span class="figure">30</span> days. <a href="#low-battery">View fix →</a></li>
<li>Yellow light twice every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, and <span class="figure">2</span> chirps. Fault. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Red light once every <span class="figure">8</span> seconds, no sound. Hush for about <span class="figure">8</span> minutes.</li>
<li>Red light once every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, no sound. The chirp is paused for about <span class="figure">10</span> hours during a low battery or a fault. The chirp comes back after that.</li>
<li>No light, <span class="figure">2</span> quick chirps a second. Another alarm started this one by radio.</li>
<li>Red light <span class="figure">3</span> times every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, no sound. This alarm was triggered within <span class="figure">72</span> hours.</li>
<li>Each red flash and each chirp. One past alarm. Event memory holds up to <span class="figure">20</span> and clears after <span class="figure">14</span> days.</li>
<li>No red flash and no chirp. No previous event.</li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> red flash and <span class="figure">1</span> chirp. Previous low battery.</li>
<li><span class="figure">2</span> red flashes and <span class="figure">2</span> chirps. Previous fault.</li>
<li><span class="figure">3</span> red flashes and <span class="figure">3</span> chirps. An alarm was triggered.</li>
</ul>
