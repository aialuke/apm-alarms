<ul class="signals">
<li>Light flashes once every <span class="figure">40</span> seconds. Standby.</li>
<li>Light stays on for <span class="figure">30</span> seconds. Pairing.</li>
<li>Light flashes briefly and then stays on. The pair worked.</li>
<li>Red light turns off. Pairing has ended. <a href="{{ '/' | append: page.brand | append: '/' | append: page.unit | append: '/troubleshooting/pairing/' | relative_url }}">Open Pairing</a></li>
<li>Light flashes for <span class="figure">8</span> seconds while the alarms start, and the smoke alarms sound for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. After a test press.</li>
<li>Light flashes for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes. That test worked.</li>
<li>Light flashes for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. Hush. The smoke alarms stay quiet for <span class="figure">10</span> minutes.</li>
<li>Light flashes, and the smoke alarms sound. SOS for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes.</li>
<li>Light does not flash when test is pressed. Report faulty.</li>
</ul>
