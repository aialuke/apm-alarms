topic: opening
---

Press the slide lock at the arrow points, then slide the alarm out of the base.

Line up the TO OPEN mark on the alarm with the slide lock on the base.

Slide the alarm and push it until it clicks.

<p class="tip">The alarm will not slide onto the base until a battery is fitted. Forcing it on without a battery can damage the alarm.</p>

The alarm is on when the base is properly engaged.
