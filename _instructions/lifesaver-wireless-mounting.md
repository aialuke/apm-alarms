---

Fix the mount to the ceiling with the screws and cavity fixings in the pack. Use a <span class="figure">5</span> mm drill bit for the cavity fixings.

Put the alarm on the mount and turn it gently clockwise, the way the cover shows, until it snaps and chirps once. The alarm is on.

The alarm fits that mount in only one alignment. Line the arrow up with the mark on the side of the alarm.

<p class="tip">In a hallway, the A line should sit at right angles to the hallway.</p>

<p class="tip">For the tamper lock, break the small middle tab off both tamper tabs, then turn the alarm onto the mount until the tamper tab snaps and locks. To take it off after that, press the tamper tab down and turn the alarm the way the arrows on the cover show.</p>
