topic: mounting
---

Twist the mount off the alarm anti-clockwise.

Place the mount on the ceiling and mark the two screw holes.

Drill the marks with a <span class="figure">5.0</span> mm bit. Keep clear of wiring in the ceiling.

Push the supplied plastic anchors into the holes, and screw the mount to the ceiling.

<p class="tip">If the alarms use a radio link, pick one side of the building, such as the front wall, and fit every mount the same way. The large hole goes nearest that wall.</p>

Line the alarm up on the mount, press down gently, and twist it on.

<p class="tip">To make the alarm hard to take off, break off the small pillar on the back. After that, push the catch towards the ceiling with a screwdriver, then twist the alarm off. A No. 2 or No. 4 self tapping screw, <span class="figure">2</span> to <span class="figure">3</span> mm across and <span class="figure">6</span> to <span class="figure">8</span> mm long, can lock the alarm to the mount. That screw is not supplied. Fit the alarm to the mount before that screw.</p>
