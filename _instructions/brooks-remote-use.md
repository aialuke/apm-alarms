---

Press and hold the front button until the TEST part lights blue. Let go when every alarm is sounding.

The alarms stop after a short time. The TEST part flashes blue when the test is finished.

For silence, wait until the SILENCE part is flashing red, then press the button. The part turns blue.

After a delay the alarms stop. All the parts flash green for a moment. The remote is in standby.
