<ul class="signals">
<li>Green light stays off and the red light flashes once every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, with no sound. Standing by on the backup battery only. Still needs live mains.</li>
<li>Green light stays on and the red light flashes once every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds, with no sound. Mains is on. The alarm and the backup battery are standing by.</li>
<li>Flashing red light during a false alarm, or just after it. This alarm set the others off.</li>
<li>Red light flashes quickly in time with a quick chirp. Test. Both stop when you let go.</li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> short chirp in time with <span class="figure">1</span> red flash every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds. Low battery. <a href="#low-battery">View fix →</a></li>
<li>Red light flashes every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds for <span class="figure">72</span> hours, with no sound. That alarm has been activated. It then returns to standby on its own.</li>
<li><span class="figure">2</span> short red flashes and <span class="figure">2</span> short chirps every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds. Fault. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Red light flashes <span class="figure">3</span> times a second, with no sound. Wiring is wrong. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Solid red light on the front for <span class="figure">2.5</span> minutes. Pairing.</li>
<li><span class="figure">5</span> flashes of the red light on a joining alarm. The radio link succeeded.</li>
<li>Low battery chirp or fault chirp stops for <span class="figure">10</span> hours after Test/Hush is held for <span class="figure">1</span> second. The light keeps working during that time.</li>
<li>Red light flashes quickly and the alarm chirps. Smoke is sensed during the quiet time after Test/Hush.</li>
</ul>
