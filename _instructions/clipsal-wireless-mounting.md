topic: mounting
---

Fit the mount with the supplied fasteners.

Line the alarm up with the clips on the mount.

Turn the alarm clockwise until it snaps into place. The alarm is on.

<p class="tip">Leave the dust cover on until you are ready to test. If the alarm does not sound when tested, fit it on the mount and turn the body clockwise until you hear a click.</p>
