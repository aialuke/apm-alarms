Hold Test for <span class="figure">3</span> cycles of the alarm sound.

This alarm sounds. The red light flashes once every <span class="figure">0.5</span> seconds. When you let go, this alarm stops sounding and its red light stops.

Check that all the other alarms sound. When you let go, they should stop sounding.

<p class="tip">If these alarms are interconnected with an RF module, hold Test for <span class="figure">3</span> cycles of the alarm sound. The blue light on the cover comes on.</p>
