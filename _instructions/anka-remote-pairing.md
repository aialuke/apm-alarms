Put the units within <span class="figure">1</span> metre of each other.

Press and hold HUSH on the remote. Let go when the green light flashes for the third time.

The red light keeps flashing for about <span class="figure">1</span> minute.

The other units pair on their own. <span class="figure">1</span> chirp and a long green light.

<p class="tip">A unit already in another group chirps quickly and flashes green. Press and hold that unit's button and let go after the green light flashes once. A chirp. It has joined.</p>

When pairing is finished, press the test button on the remote once. The other units chirp once more. The green light turns off.

Press the test button on the remote once more. After about <span class="figure">3</span> to <span class="figure">4</span> seconds the paired units sound one by one.

<p class="tip">That sound is loud.</p>
