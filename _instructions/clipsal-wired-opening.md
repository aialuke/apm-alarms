topic: opening
---

Unlock the alarm from the base, then unclip it.

Clip the alarm to the base.

Turn the power switch to the I position. Use light pressure to lock the alarm onto the base.

If the alarm does not sound when tested, put it on the base and turn it clockwise until you hear a click.

Turn the power on. Green light. The alarm has mains power.
