Take the protective film off the battery before use.

Press NET once. The light stays on for <span class="figure">30</span> seconds.

To leave, press NET again. The red light going off means pairing is off.

While that light is on, press TEST on the alarm <span class="figure">3</span> times within <span class="figure">3</span> seconds.

Do that for every alarm. The remote connects to each alarm on its own.

If the pair works, the light flashes briefly and then stays on.
