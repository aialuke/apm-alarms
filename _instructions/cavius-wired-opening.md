topic: opening
---

Twist the alarm counter-clockwise off the base.

<p class="tip">If the safety lock pin is fitted, pop it out with the pointed end of the tab before that twist.</p>

Line up the two lines on the alarm and the base, then turn clockwise until it clicks.

<p class="tip">If the battery is missing or fitted the wrong way, the alarm will not click onto the base.</p>
