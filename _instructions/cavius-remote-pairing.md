Slide the switch on the remote to Learn mode. Put the other wireless alarms into Learn mode as well.

The torch lights.

Press and hold the test button on the remote. Sharp chirp while it flashes.

The other alarms flash red. When every wireless alarm is flashing, they are connected.

Flick the switches back to normal. The alarms stop flashing.
