The end button is test and hush.

Hold the red end button for two sweeps of the alarms, until the remote stops flashing, sounding, and vibrating. The remote sends a test. During the test it flashes and sounds.

If the remote receives a test from another unit, it flashes and sounds. A press of the end button stops that.

When a connected alarm sounds, the torch flashes rapidly. The vibration and the sound follow the alarm that started it.

In the first <span class="figure">30</span> seconds of an alarm, one press of the red end button stops the remote sounding and vibrating. The light keeps flashing. Hush then starts by itself within <span class="figure">30</span> seconds.

After <span class="figure">30</span> seconds of an alarm, one press sends hush for <span class="figure">13</span> seconds. That press can be repeated. The remote can stop the alarm only after those <span class="figure">30</span> seconds.

Stay in the same room as the alarm, within <span class="figure">4</span> metres. Hushing the alarm that is flashing silences every alarm at once.

The side button turns the torch on and off. The torch turns off by itself after <span class="figure">5</span> minutes. During an alarm, the side button changes the torch from a flash to a steady light.
