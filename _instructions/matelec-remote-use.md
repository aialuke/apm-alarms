One press of Test makes the light flash for <span class="figure">8</span> seconds. The alarms sound for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. The light then flashes for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes when the test worked.

While the alarms are sounding, one press of Hush makes the light flash for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. The alarms then stay quiet for <span class="figure">10</span> minutes.

One press of SOS makes the light flash. The alarms then sound for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes.
