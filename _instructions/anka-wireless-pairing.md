On the master, press and hold the test button until the green light has flashed <span class="figure">3</span> times. The red light then flashes. That is pairing mode for up to <span class="figure">60</span> seconds.

On each other alarm, press and hold the test button until the green light has flashed <span class="figure">2</span> times. The red light flashes while it learns. The green light flashes <span class="figure">3</span> times when it has paired.
