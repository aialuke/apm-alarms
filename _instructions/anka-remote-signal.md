<ul class="signals">
<li>Red light keeps flashing for about <span class="figure">1</span> minute after the third green flash. Main unit for pairing. <a href="{{ '/' | append: page.brand | append: '/' | append: page.unit | append: '/troubleshooting/pairing/' | relative_url }}">Open Pairing</a></li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> chirp and a long green light. That unit is pairing.</li>
<li>Chirps quickly and flashes green. Already in another group.</li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> chirp after <span class="figure">1</span> green flash. That unit has joined.</li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> chirp and the green light turns off on the other unit. Pairing is finished.</li>
<li>Chirp <span class="figure">3</span> times a minute. First alarm that activated.</li>
<li>Alarm sound for <span class="figure">10</span> to <span class="figure">15</span> seconds, then the alarm stops. Test.</li>
</ul>
