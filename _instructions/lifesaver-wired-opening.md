topic: opening
---

Turn the mains off before the alarm comes off the base.

Find the words SLIDE TO REMOVE. Take out the tamper locking screw if one is fitted.

Hold the back of the base and slide the alarm the way the arrow shows. Push firmly that way until the alarm is free of the base.

Line the alarm up with the base. Slide it on, then slide it the opposite way to the remove arrow. Push it toward the connector marked A until it slides fully in.

<p class="tip">On a wall, the connector must be at the bottom, and the SLIDE TO REMOVE arrow must point up.</p>

Fit the tamper locking screw if you want that lock.

When mains is on, the green light comes on if the alarm is properly on the base.
