---
brand: brooks
unit: wired
topic: opening
---

Turn the mains off before the alarm comes off the base.

Find the arrow on the front of the alarm. The removal slot is directly above that arrow.

Put a flat-bladed screwdriver in horizontally, about <span class="figure">10</span> mm, into the centre of the slot.

Keep the screwdriver in place, and push the lower half of the alarm away from the screwdriver, following the arrows on the cover.

Hold the lower half and lower the alarm off the base towards the floor.

Line it up on the base and slide it on. Mains power and the battery connect as it slides on.
