<ul class="signals">
<li>Green light once a minute. Standby.</li>
<li>Flashing red during a false alarm, or just after it. This alarm set the others off.</li>
<li>Red light twice a minute. Fault. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Sounds again after <span class="figure">9</span> minutes. Smoke is still present.</li>
<li>Red light once with a short chirp, then <span class="figure">3</span> chirps while the red light flashes <span class="figure">3</span> times, for <span class="figure">2</span> cycles. Single test.</li>
<li>Chirps the whole time and the red light flashes. The alarm you hold, on an interconnected test.</li>
<li>Chirp, and the lights flash red and green in turn. The other alarms, on an interconnected test.</li>
<li>The other alarms leave the test after a few seconds. Interconnected test.</li>
<li>Red flash and <span class="figure">1</span> chirp every <span class="figure">60</span> seconds. Low battery until the battery is depleted. <a href="#low-battery">View fix →</a></li>
<li>Chirp on and off. Clean the outside vents.</li>
</ul>
