<ul class="signals">
<li>Sounds as though smoke is present. The alarm you test. Test press. The alarm is working.</li>
<li>Sound a few moments after the alarm you test. The other interconnected alarms.</li>
<li>Silence for <span class="figure">10</span> minutes. Then the alarm goes back to normal use.</li>
<li>Flashing red during a false alarm, or just after it. This alarm set the others off.</li>
</ul>
