topic: opening
---

Find the arrow and the slot above it on the front of the alarm.

Put a flat-bladed screwdriver in horizontally, about <span class="figure">10</span> mm, into the centre of the slot.

Push the lower half of the alarm away from the screwdriver.

Hold the lower half and take the alarm off the base.

Line the alarm up with the base and slide it on the way the base shows.

<p class="tip">The foam ceiling gasket must be in place.</p>
