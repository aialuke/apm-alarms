Press and hold Silence for <span class="figure">5</span> seconds, until the red light and the yellow light flash at the same time, then let go.

Do this only the first time the remote is used.

On the alarm, press the test button <span class="figure">3</span> times within <span class="figure">2</span> seconds, until the red light flashes.

Press Silence on the middle of the remote <span class="figure">3</span> times within <span class="figure">2</span> seconds.

Do that again for any other alarm or remote that should join.
