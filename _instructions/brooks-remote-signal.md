<ul class="signals">
<li>CO indicator. CO alarm.</li>
<li>Low battery indicator. Remote battery has reached the end of its life. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Fire, battery, and CO indicators flash. Power up.</li>
<li>Each part lights red, then blue, then green. Power up.</li>
<li>Every light goes off. Standby.</li>
<li>Number of blue flashes equals the number of radio alarms and remotes, and that pattern repeats every <span class="figure">5</span> to <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. Pairing. <a href="{{ '/' | append: page.brand | append: '/' | append: page.unit | append: '/troubleshooting/pairing/' | relative_url }}">Open Pairing</a></li>
<li>Test part lights blue. Radio test has started.</li>
<li>Test part flashes blue. Radio test has finished.</li>
<li>Fire icon or the CO icon flashes rapidly for <span class="figure">2</span> minutes. An alarm started and then returned to standby.</li>
<li>CO icon then flashes once every <span class="figure">60</span> seconds for <span class="figure">24</span> hours. After a CO alarm.</li>
<li>Green flash on the memory part, and the fire indicator or the CO indicator also flashes. Alarm memory is set, in the diagnostic check.</li>
<li>Test part flashes blue during the <span class="figure">2</span> minute diagnostic test. Refresh signal is being sent.</li>
<li>All parts flash green. Diagnostic test is finished.</li>
</ul>
