{% include pairing-start.html %}

Press the test button <span class="figure">3</span> times on the front. Turn the alarm over. A green light on the back means it is in pairing mode. That light then changes to blue while it searches for the other alarms.

On each other alarm, press the test button <span class="figure">3</span> times.

When the first alarm connects, its light turns green. Each other alarm shows a green light on the back when it is connected.
