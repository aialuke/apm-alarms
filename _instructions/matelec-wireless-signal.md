<ul class="signals">
<li>Low red flash every <span class="figure">40</span> seconds and no sound. Normal running.</li>
<li>Red flash and <span class="figure">1</span> chirp every <span class="figure">40</span> seconds. Low battery. <a href="#low-battery">View fix →</a></li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> chirp every <span class="figure">40</span> seconds, with no red flash. Internal fault. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Red flash once every <span class="figure">8</span> seconds and no sound. Hush for about <span class="figure">10</span> minutes.</li>
<li>Flashing red light during a false alarm, or just after it. This alarm set the others off.</li>
<li>Alarm, alarm, then a <span class="figure">1</span> second pause, and no red flash. A paired alarm that did not detect the smoke.</li>
<li><span class="figure">1</span> red flash and <span class="figure">1</span> chirp while the button is held. Local test.</li>
<li><span class="figure">3</span> short chirps, a <span class="figure">1.5</span> second pause, and the same again, until you let go. Local test.</li>
<li>Red light flashes twice a second for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes, or until CONTROL is pressed again. Paired alarm during a radio test.</li>
<li><span class="figure">2</span> chirps every <span class="figure">1</span> second for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. Paired alarm during a radio test.</li>
<li><span class="figure">2</span> flashes about every <span class="figure">20</span> seconds. The alarm that was triggered. One timing is up to <span class="figure">168</span> hours. The other is <span class="figure">72</span> hours.</li>
<li>Red network light at the back. No network yet.</li>
<li>Blue network light at the back. That alarm is sending a pair request.</li>
<li>Green network light for <span class="figure">3</span> seconds. The pair worked.</li>
<li>Blue network light stays on until another pair request. After a good pair, on the alarm you started with.</li>
<li>Blue light turns off. Pairing has ended.</li>
</ul>
