Press and hold the test button on any alarm for at least <span class="figure">10</span> seconds.

The alarms then give a short chirp, and the light flashes every <span class="figure">8</span> seconds for <span class="figure">2</span> minutes.

<span class="figure">1</span> chirp every <span class="figure">8</span> seconds. Connected and working.

<span class="figure">3</span> short chirps every <span class="figure">8</span> seconds. Smoke sensor fault. Report faulty.

A short press of the test button stops that early on that alarm.

<p class="tip">After a test, every Cavius alarm chirps for about <span class="figure">2</span> minutes. That chirp is normal. Wait for it to finish, then wait another <span class="figure">2</span> minutes for any further chirp.</p>
