If you test straight after the alarm is first turned on, wait a few seconds for it to settle.

<p class="lead">One alarm</p>

Press Test/Silence.

The red light flashes once with a short chirp. The alarm then chirps <span class="figure">3</span> times, and the red light flashes <span class="figure">3</span> times, for <span class="figure">2</span> cycles.

<p class="lead">Interconnected alarms</p>

Press and hold Test/Silence on any interconnected alarm until the other alarms start to chirp.

This alarm chirps the whole time you hold the button. Its red light flashes.

Lights on the other alarms flash red and yellow, one then the other. When you let go, this alarm stops. The other alarms stop soon after.

<p class="lead">Just after mains power is connected</p>

Hold Test/Silence for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. This alarm chirps. The other GT alarms also chirp during that hold. When you let go, the sound stops at once.
