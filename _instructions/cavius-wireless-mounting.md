<p class="lead">Standard mount</p>

Use the mount ring to mark the screw holes on the ceiling.

Fix that mount with the enclosed screws and plugs.

Click the alarm firmly into the mount.

Detach the safety lock pin from the tab and push it into the safety lock slot.

<p class="lead">Recess mount</p>

Place the cover ring on the spot and trace around the inside of the ring.

Cut the hole with a hole saw of <span class="figure">81</span> mm to <span class="figure">85</span> mm.

Put the recess mount in the hole and secure it with screws.

Click the alarm firmly into that mount. Place the cover ring around it.

<p class="tip">The safety lock cannot be used with the recess mount. A spare pin is in the pack. To take the pin out, use the pointed end of the tab to pop it out.</p>
