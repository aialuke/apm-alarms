The alarm must be on mains power, and the green light must be on.

<p class="lead">One alarm</p>

Push Hush/Test for <span class="figure">2</span> to <span class="figure">3</span> seconds.

This alarm gives <span class="figure">3</span> chirps in <span class="figure">4</span> seconds, and the red light flashes <span class="figure">3</span> times every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds. Both repeat until you let go.

<p class="lead">This alarm and the others</p>

Push Hush/Test for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. The horn on this alarm keeps sounding until you let go.

This alarm gives <span class="figure">3</span> chirps in <span class="figure">4</span> seconds, and the red light flashes <span class="figure">3</span> times every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds. Both repeat until you let go.

The other interconnected alarms give <span class="figure">3</span> chirps in <span class="figure">2</span> seconds. That repeats until you let go. On the radio alarms, the blue light flashes once every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds and stays on for <span class="figure">3</span> minutes.

After this test, the alarm is less sensitive to smoke for about <span class="figure">20</span> minutes. Wait <span class="figure">20</span> minutes before a smoke spray test.

<p class="lead">Radio range</p>

Hold Network on one radio alarm for <span class="figure">10</span> to <span class="figure">12</span> seconds. The green light must be on before you start.

This alarm shows a solid blue light. That mode ends by itself after <span class="figure">9</span> minutes.

Wait <span class="figure">30</span> seconds, then look at the other radio alarms. They show a slow blue flash. That also ends after <span class="figure">9</span> minutes.

To leave, hold Network on the alarm you started with and count <span class="figure">2</span> to <span class="figure">3</span> blue flashes, then let go. The blue light on the alarm you started with flashes fast. The blue lights on the other alarms turn off. Closing can take up to <span class="figure">90</span> seconds.
