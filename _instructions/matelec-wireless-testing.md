Hold CONTROL on any alarm for <span class="figure">3</span> cycles of the alarm sound.

While CONTROL is held on this alarm, the light flashes once and the alarm chirps once. The alarm can also sound <span class="figure">3</span> short chirps, then a <span class="figure">1.5</span> second pause, and repeat until you let go.

After a short delay, every paired alarm sounds for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. On those alarms the red light flashes twice a second for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes, or until CONTROL is pressed again. They chirp twice every <span class="figure">1</span> second for those <span class="figure">10</span> seconds.

Do not test again for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes.

<p class="tip">The alarms cannot be hushed while a test is running.</p>
