Slide the switch on the back of each alarm to the Learn Mode position.

Press the button on the top of one alarm only. The other alarms stay in Learn Mode.

The red light flashes on that alarm. Each other alarm flashes its light.

When every alarm is flashing the red light, they are connected. Switch them out of Learn Mode.
