<ul class="signals">
<li>Torch changes from a flash to a steady light. Press the black side button.</li>
<li>Remote stops sounding and stops vibrating. One press of the red test and hush button in the first <span class="figure">30</span> seconds of an alarm.</li>
<li>Light keeps flashing. After that press.</li>
<li>Remote sends hush by itself within <span class="figure">30</span> seconds. After that press.</li>
<li>Hush signal for <span class="figure">13</span> seconds. One press after <span class="figure">30</span> seconds of an alarm. The remote can stop the alarm that started it only after those <span class="figure">30</span> seconds.</li>
<li>Flashing only on the alarm that first sensed the smoke.</li>
<li>Short chirp every <span class="figure">48</span> seconds. Low battery. Only the remote chirps. The smoke alarms do not chirp. Report faulty.</li>
<li>Torch off after <span class="figure">5</span> minutes. Turns off by itself.</li>
</ul>
