<p class="lead">One alarm</p>

Press and hold Test/Hush for more than <span class="figure">3</span> seconds, until the alarm sounds. Do not press it again and again.

The standby light blinks red once every <span class="figure">1</span> second. The sound is <span class="figure">3</span> chirps every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds. Both keep going until you let go.

If there is no sound, check the power switch is on, then press Test/Hush until the alarm sounds. If there is still no sound, report faulty.

<p class="lead">Interconnected alarms</p>

On any interconnected alarm, press and hold Test/Hush for more than <span class="figure">10</span> seconds, until an alarm goes off on all of them.

On this test the standby light stays off. The sound is <span class="figure">3</span> chirps every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds. The other alarms sound for <span class="figure">25</span> seconds. Their sound keeps going for longer after you let go.
