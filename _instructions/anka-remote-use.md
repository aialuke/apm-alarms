To test, stand within <span class="figure">3</span> metres, point the remote at the alarm, and press TEST once.

Wait <span class="figure">3</span> to <span class="figure">5</span> seconds. The alarm should sound for <span class="figure">10</span> to <span class="figure">15</span> seconds and then stop.

Do not press any button on the remote or the alarm during that test.

Press HUSH once to silence a nuisance alarm. If an alarm has already set the others off, HUSH mutes all of them.
