Turn the mount to the ON position. The alarm turns on.

Turn the mount to the OFF position. The alarm turns off.

Putting the alarm on the mount and turning it clockwise until it clicks also turns it on. <a href="{{ '/' | append: page.brand | append: '/' | append: page.unit | append: '/setup/mounting/' | relative_url }}">Open Mounting</a>
