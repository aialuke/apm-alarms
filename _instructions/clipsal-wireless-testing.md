Press Test/Hush on each alarm, one alarm at a time. Do not use an open flame.

Hold Test/Hush for <span class="figure">3</span> seconds, until the second burst of <span class="figure">3</span> chirps has finished.

On the alarm you are holding, the red light flashes once every <span class="figure">1</span> second. The sound is <span class="figure">3</span> chirps every <span class="figure">4</span> seconds. Both last until you let go.

When you let go, this alarm sends the radio signal. All the interconnected alarms sound for <span class="figure">10</span> seconds. Their red light flashes twice a second for <span class="figure">5</span> minutes. During that flash they also chirp twice every <span class="figure">1</span> second.

Hold Test/Hush on one alarm until it sounds, and check that the red light is flashing on all the interconnected alarms.

After the first alarm, press Test/Hush briefly on the next alarm to stop the flashing red light. Then hold Test/Hush again until that alarm sounds. Do this on every alarm.
