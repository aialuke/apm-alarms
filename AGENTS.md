# Agent notes

- The owner is a smoke alarm technician, not a developer. Talk to them in plain language, and use `/bro` if you start sounding technical.
- The pstack skills and agents live in `.cursor/skills/` and `.cursor/agents/`. Most are only run when called by name, such as `/poteto-mode` or `/interrogate`. `/poteto-mode` links to the rest, so read `.cursor/skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md` in full before working in that style.
- To see or click the phone page, follow `.cursor/skills/verify-apm-alarms/SKILL.md`. That look uses the Chrome DevTools plugin on the local site. browser-use stays for other people’s websites.
- Claude and GPT are usage-blocked. Do not write them into the model rule even if the tool lists the slugs.
- The topic map names the button and the page heading. That heading is the current step of the route, written once. The file that holds the words does not. A gold count and a link sit in the sentence, beside the blue step number. Steps sit in cards with the same tight blue edge as the signal table. A heading starts a card, a page with no heading is one card, and numbers restart in each card. The last "If ..." lines are unnumbered outcome rows. The cards are built in `_plugins/screens.rb`, so do not write card markup by hand. A Remote is one page with a card for Activation, Pairing and Use. It has no light and sound table. It has no Setup or Troubleshooting list.
- What's that light or sound? is a table. The lamp is the color. The sound cell is the timing. The table is one rounded card with the tight blue edge. The header and the dashes are centred, and the meaning is centred in white under each row. A faint line marks the cells. Pages list their rows as `signals:` in front matter, drawn by `_includes/signal-table.html`. Do not write table markup by hand. The writing rules are in `docs/product-brief.md`.
- The app is dark only. It ignores the phone’s light setting. There is no theme switch. The type is the iPhone system font. The ground is near-black. Brand names stay large rounded buttons, three across, with a tight blue edge. Unit, Setup or Troubleshooting, and Topics are rows on the black page with a grey line between them, and a blue icon where the rows are different kinds of thing. Topic rows have no icon. Back and Home stay on every screen after Brands, in a bar at the bottom with the same tight blue edge. A Tip is a rounded inset note with that edge, and so is the signal table. Inside a step card a Tip is a tinted row, not a second card. A clearance, a wait, a press count, or a confirmed flash interval is gold. The words around that number stay white.
